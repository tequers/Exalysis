What to notice

Snap x to |0⟩, then to |1⟩. Where does Mx land — and did you multiply anything?
Onto the columns. M|0⟩ is column 1, M|1⟩ is column 2. So H|1⟩ = column 2 of H = |−⟩, read straight off.
Load H and sweep θ all the way round. Which number refuses to move?
|Mx|. That is what M†M = I buys: unit vector in, unit vector out — a valid state stays valid.
Still on H: hunt for a θ where Mx lies on the dashed violet line. How many are there?
Two, at 22.5° and 112.5° — the eigenvectors, with λ = +1 and λ = −1. H leaves one alone and flips the other.
Load Rotate 30° and hunt again.
Nothing lines up. A rotation fixes no real direction; its eigenvalues are complex.
Drag column 2 on top of column 1.
|det| = 0: the plane squashes onto a line, so many inputs share one output and no inverse exists.
